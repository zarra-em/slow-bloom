**INGREDIENTS**
=
**COOKING POT**
- **butter and olive oil**
- **2 large leeks** (caramelized)
- **white wine**
- **2.5 cups of stock** 
- **3-4 russet potatoes**
- **1 bay leaf**
- **2 cups heavy cream**
- **nutmeg**
- **parmesan**
- **salt**

**STEPS**
=
1. peel off the first layer, cut off the dark green part of the leeks, and put them aside (you can use the dark leaves for vegetable stocks, but you won't be adding them to the pot at this stage). slice up the white/pale green part of the leek (make sure it's thoroughly washed because leeks have a lot of sand embedded in their crevices). peel and slice the potatoes (mise en place!!), measure out the stock, and measure out the cream.
2. add a copious amount of olive oil and a dab of butter to the cooking pot. turn the stove on to medium heat and add in the chopped leeks. cook for about 20 minutes (or until the leeks are caramelized).
3. once the leeks are caramelized, add in the white wine to deglaze the pot (dry is best). add in the stock, the sliced chunks of potatoes, and a bay leaf.
4. when the potatoes become soft, add the soup mixture into a blender. blend until smooth.
5. add in the heavy cream (after tempering it).
6. add salt to taste. 
7. serve hot or cold with grated nutmeg and/or parmesan (to taste).
